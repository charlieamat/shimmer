Confluence board — concentric ring concepts (sketches, not wired into the prototype)

Per Polak's note ("Prototype, so ring size shouldn't change, but concentric OR multiple rings added") — three rough ways to represent MORE than a handful of node-groups without inflating a single ring's radius. These are static illustrations to react to, not functional builds — none of the three is picked yet.

A — Concentric, going inward

Each group is still one ring at a fixed size. As a category/Potential grows past its first ring's capacity, additional rings nest INSIDE it, shrinking toward the center. Overlap logic with neighbors stays on the outermost ring only.

B — Concentric, going outward

Same idea, reversed: the first ring stays fixed-size at the group's "home" radius, and extra rings for that same group ring OUTWARD from it as investment grows, pushing further from center.

C — No concentric rings, just more of them

No nesting at all — ring size never changes. Instead, once a group needs more capacity than one ring holds, a whole SEPARATE flat ring is added to the pentagon/hex layout alongside the others, overlapping its own neighbors independently (same rule as today's 5-ring layout, just with more than 5 rings in play).
