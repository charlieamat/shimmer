Pitch

Single-entity roguelite where you generate a fully custom character through a series of conceptual choices, then progress that character across runs. Long-term ambition is an MMO roguelike/RPG; the actual starting scope is a much simpler single-player auto-battler to prove out the character-generation and progression systems first.

Setting / Lore (light, expand later)

  • You play as a Blank — an entity that does not exist in all realities. Everyone else exists as different versions of themselves across infinite realities; you are unique and singular, and can traverse realities without ever meeting yourself.
  • You choose two Potentials (thematic power sources, e.g. Earth + Honor) — this is what "un-blanks" you for a run.
  • The Shimmer — a pursuing boundary/wall (FTL's rebel fleet, functionally). Getting caught by it shunts you back to your blank, non-existent state — i.e. run failure.
  • Meta-structure is FTL-like: a branching node map, jump forward, can backtrack, but the Shimmer forces overall forward progress.

Character Creation Flow

Step 1 — Pick two Potentials

Thematic pair (e.g. Earth + Honor) that seeds everything downstream. Not every Potential combination will be supported at launch — deliberately scoped down; UI shows only valid combos for what's been built.

Step 2 — Craft 8 Abilities

Exactly 8 abilities, each defined by three choices:

  • Aspect (flavor/power source): one of 8 — Cunning, Desire, Flow, Impetus, Presence, Spirit, Tenacity, Will. Each Aspect can be used once — picking one for an ability removes it from the pool for the rest of the build.
  • Category: Combat (direct damage), Support (buffs allies / hinders enemies), or Utility (buffs / hard CC). Max 4 of your 8 abilities can share a Category.
  • Delivery type: Single Target vs. Area Effect. Visually: shape = Category (arrow = Combat, circle = Support, diamond = Utility), orientation = targeting.

Locking in a choice auto-generates the ability's name and preview. During a run, abilities are manually triggered on cooldown on top of passive auto-battling.

Step 3 — Vocations (talent resource generation)

Every ability crafted also generates one Vocation (talent) — not slotted immediately, banked as a resource for the Confluence Board. A Vocation is derived from: Potential 1 + Potential 2 + Aspect + delivery type.

Synergy scaling: the more abilities you stack into one Category, the more bonus Vocations you generate for the Aspects used in that Category — not just 1-per-ability. Heavy investment in one Category means a deep Vocation pool for that Category's Aspects, at the cost of fewer Vocations elsewhere.

Step 4 — Confluence Board (talent board)

A Diablo 4 Paragon Board homage: a node board you snake a path through, placing generated Vocations as you go.

  • Starting board is triangular, divided into thirds — each third weighted by two of the three Categories, shaped by how many abilities you put into each.
  • Reaching the board's edge lets you attach and rotate another board ("Confluence Page") to keep snaking.
  • New Confluence Pages are earned via Rune Words — words/knowledge collected while adventuring across realities. Stronger Rune Words → boards that reach an edge faster with fewer nodes. Weaker ones → more nodes, more incremental power.
  • Making a board connection unlocks a Vestige (item) slot.

Step 5 — Vestiges (items)

  • No traditional gear slots — simplified slot model.
  • Base board always grants 1 Vestige slot; each connection adds another.
  • Stat scaling is threshold-based, not flat %: e.g. 30 rating = +1 hit, 60 = +2 hits, thresholds grow from there.
  • You don't find finished items — you find Orbs with set stats, and socket them onto a Vestige to raise its level, up to a cap gated by character progression.
  • Vestiges are fully re-craftable — no regret cost for leveling an item for a build you later drop.

Progression / Loadouts

  • Multiple loadouts (different Potential pairs), switched at a home base between runs.
  • Speculative leveling model: level cap 60 total, split 30/30 across your two active Potentials. Progress on a shared Potential carries over across loadouts that reuse it.
  • Combinatorics are intentionally large — different player goals (maximize distinct Vocations used vs. minmax efficiency) are expected to emerge organically rather than every combination being meaningfully distinct by design.

Explicitly Unresolved / Out of Scope for v1

  • Full Potential combination matrix (only a subset buildable initially).
  • Exact Aspect/Category/Type → Vocation generation formula (illustrative, not final).
  • Confluence Board node layout details, rotation mechanics, exact Rune-Word-to-board-size mapping.
  • Full item/Orb scaling system.
  • Leveling system (level cap, XP, etc.).
  • Vestige slot cap — placeholder number only.
  • Deeper lore beyond Blank / Shimmer / Potentials premise.

This Prototype's Scope

Hardcodes 1 Potential pair, all 8 Aspects, the 3 Categories, and a minimal Confluence Board (single triangular board, no Rune Words/multi-board) — enough to click through ability creation → Vocation generation → placing Vocations on a board. Vestiges/items and the FTL meta-map are intentionally skipped; they're separable systems. This validates the generation logic, which is the actual novel part of the design, before investing in content tooling or a real engine.
